How to use the DM-12

Pads: click or tap them, or use the keys Q–Y (top row) and A–H (bottom row). Hold Shift for a harder hit. Space starts and stops.

Step Sequencer: click a step to turn it on or off, or drag across a row to paint. Shift-click (or Accent mode) makes a louder accent step. Click a track name to hear it. M mutes a track, S solos it.

Loop Recorder: pick a length and quantise, press REC (or Enter), then play the pads as the loop goes round. Overdub adds hits each pass; Replace swaps out a drum's old hits. The slider next to Quantise sets its strength, and Apply re-snaps the whole loop. Click an empty spot in a lane to add a hit, or click a hit to remove it. ⌘/Ctrl+Z undoes. To erase while playing, hold Delete plus a pad key (or switch on Erase and hold a pad).

Your current beat and loop are kept automatically. Use Save… to keep copies by name.
